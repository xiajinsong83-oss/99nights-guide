How to Redeem Codes in the Lobby

1

Launch the game and stay in the Lobby

2

Click the diamond icon in the bottom-left corner

3

Press the blue Codes button in the bottom-right

4

Enter the code and hit Submit to claim

💡 Special case: "yay fishing" is NOT redeemed in the lobby. First upgrade your Campfire to level 2 and obtain the fishing rod. While the rod is in the water at a fishing spot, press / to open chat and type yay fishing (with the space) to instantly spawn 2 Gems.
