99 Nights in the Forest FAQ — Platforms, Players, Movie & More

Quick answers about 99 Nights in the Forest: is it free, what platforms, how many players, can you play solo, is it based on a movie, and how do you rescue the children.

Home/99 Nights in the Forest FAQ — Platforms, Players, Movie & More

Quick answers for new players. If you don’t see your question here, the guides section probably covers it.

Is 99 Nights in the Forest free to play?
Yes. It's a free Roblox game. Some classes and cosmetics cost Gems or Robux, but the core game and all progression are free.
What platform can I play it on?
It runs wherever Roblox runs: PC, Mac, iOS, Android, Xbox and VR. Progress is tied to your Roblox account.
How many players can play together?
It's a co-op game designed for squads. Server capacity varies, but the game is built around groups of 2-4+ players working together.
Can I play 99 Nights in the Forest solo?
You can, but it's significantly harder. Solo players should extend the early 'core' phase, never wander at night, and rely on traps and kiting rather than direct fights.
How do I play with friends?
Join the same Roblox server: join a friend's game from the Roblox friends list, or use a server code/join link if the game provides one. Then build your camp together.
Is 99 Nights in the Forest based on a movie?
The game has a 20th Century Studios movie tie-in that has drawn in many new players. The game itself is a co-op survival horror experience on Roblox.
How do I rescue the missing children?
Explore systematically with food, weapons and light. Mark clues across the map; finding and following clues unlocks rescue runs and late-game content.
Are the codes case-sensitive?
No. Codes can be entered in any combination of upper and lower case as long as the spelling is correct — including the space in 'yay fishing'.
What is Forest Rage hard mode?
It's the late-game hard mode with higher threat scaling, the Corruption mechanic, Rift clearing and the Research Outpost objective. See the Forest Rage guide for details.
