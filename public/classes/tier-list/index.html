Best Class in 99 Nights in the Forest — Tier List (Community)

Community tier list for 99 Nights in the Forest classes: which starter class is best for solo, for squads, for new players and for hard mode — with reasoning.

Home/ Class Compendium — All 10 Classes in 99 Nights in the Forest/ Best Class in 99 Nights in the Forest — Tier List (Community)
Last updated: October 3, 2026

There is no single “best class” — but there is a best class for your situation. This tier list ranks the 10 classes by how much they contribute to a typical squad run, with notes for solo play.

⚠ Opinion, not gospel: This is a community opinion ranking based on the current class roles. Balance patches can move classes between tiers — the reasoning below matters more than the letter.

Tier list

S — Meta picks

  • Medic — healing and revives are the difference between a wipe and a recovery. Every serious squad wants one.
  • Lumberjack — the wood economy runs the whole base. Fast chopping compounds into faster campfire and bench upgrades.

A — Strong and flexible

  • Scavenger — supply running keeps the loop moving; weaker in combat-heavy squads.

  • Cook — food pressure is real on long runs; less useful in short sessions.

  • Woodsman — a second wood engine: the unique Woodsman’s Axe plus log-storage and giant-sapling perks compound into faster campfire and bench upgrades.

B — Situational

  • Ranger — shines in exploration and rescue phases, mediocre in pure defense nights.

  • Assassin — fun and strong in skilled hands, but glassy for new players.

  • Escape Artist — chase-escape and detection perks keep you alive when you don’t want to fight; shines for solo runners.

  • Elemental — biome helmet and status perks are strong when the right biome rolls, situational otherwise.

C — Learning tool

  • Camper — perfectly fine to start, but outclassed once you know the loop.

The one rule

Your squad should never have zero Medics. Healing decides night outcomes more than any other single factor.
