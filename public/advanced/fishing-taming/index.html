Fishing & Animal Taming in 99 Nights in the Forest

Fishing and animal taming in 99 Nights in the Forest: campfire level 2, the fishing rod, the yay fishing chat code trick, and how taming works.

Home/ Advanced Guides — Crafting, Flames, Fishing & Hard Mode/ Fishing & Animal Taming in 99 Nights in the Forest
Last updated: September 30, 2026

Fishing is the most famous side activity in 99 Nights in the Forest — because it hides a working chat code. Taming is the animal companion system that helps gatherers and scouts.

Fishing — unlock and use

  1. Upgrade your Campfire to level 2.
  2. Craft/obtain the fishing rod.
  3. Go to a fishing spot and cast the rod into the water.
  4. Wait for the bite, then reel in.

The yay fishing chat code

While the rod is in the water at a fishing spot, press / to open chat and type:

yay fishing

The game spawns 2 Gems instantly. The space between the words is required, and the rod must be in the water.

What fishing gives you

  • Food — a steady, renewable food source for the base.
  • Gems — via the chat code and event fish.
  • Materials — some catches convert into useful resources.

Animal taming — how it works

Taming lets you befriend animals that then help around the base. The taming loop (framework):

  1. Find the animal — taming candidates spawn around the map.
  2. Feed/approach it — taming requires the right bait or interaction.
  3. Claim it — tamed animals stay at your base and provide their role bonus.
⚠️ Verify in-game: The full taming list, bait requirements and pet roles are part of ongoing research — treat the loop above as the framework and confirm current mechanics in-game.

Quick tips

  • Fish in the daytime — night fishing pulls threats.
  • Keep one rod in the base storage so anyone can fish.
  • Tamed animals that gather save a full player’s worth of daily work.
