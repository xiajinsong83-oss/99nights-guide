99 Nights in the Forest drops you into a co-op survival loop that looks simple until it isn't: chop wood by day, keep the campfire lit by night, and rescue the missing children before the cultists figure out where you are. The game teaches almost nothing. The Deer teaches the rest the hard way. This site is our attempt to write down everything we wish we had known on night 1, from working codes that actually pay out to the base layout that stops night 7 from being your last.

We are a fan crew, not the devs. The game is made by Grandma's Favourite Games on Roblox, and the recent 20th Century Studios movie tie-in has dumped a lot of new players into the server with zero survival experience. Half the questions we see in chat are the same ten questions, so we wrote them all down instead of repeating them in every lobby.

## What changed recently — read this first

Two big patches landed in September 2026, and they move the meta more than anything this year:

- **September 19 — Class Shop Revamp.** Every class now stays in the shop permanently instead of rotating in and out of stock, and selected classes went on sale. The devs timed it with a 2x Diamond weekend (September 19–22) to celebrate the Roblox Innovation Awards 2026 Best Survival Game win, and dropped the `awardwinner_yay` code at the same time. If you held off buying a class because it might come back cheaper, that logic is dead now: what you see is what's in stock.
- **September 5 — New Classes.** Woodsman, Escape Artist and Elemental went live with daily quests and fire offerings. The Woodsman's 30-second, 40-tree chop quest is the one people rage at most.

## Where to start

If you have never played a night, read the [How to Play]({{< relref "/guide/how-to-play" >}}) loop page first, then walk into a run with the [First 10 Nights route]({{< relref "/guide/first-10-nights" >}}) open. That route is a day-by-day checklist, not a vibe: when to upgrade the campfire, when to push the crafting bench, when to stop exploring and wall up. Most squads we watch die because they skip the campfire level 2 and discover the fishing unlock three weeks later.

If you are already past night 10, jump straight to the sections that hurt:

- [Codes]({{< relref "/codes" >}}) — four working codes as of our October 3 verification, including the weird one that you type into chat instead of the lobby. Expired codes stay listed so you can tell the fake-code videos apart.
- [Classes]({{< relref "/classes" >}}) — ten starter roles and our [tier list]({{< relref "/classes/tier-list" >}}). Short version: every serious run wants a Medic, and Lumberjack runs the economy. The September 5 patches moved Woodsman and Escape Artist up the board.
- [Monsters]({{< relref "/monsters" >}}) — the Deer circles your fire, cultists raid on a rough 3–4 day timer, and the Ram will demolish the wall you are proud of. Our [Deer page]({{< relref "/monsters/deer" >}}) covers the secret scaling mechanic and the counter that stops being "run away" once you know it.
- [Advanced]({{< relref "/advanced" >}}) — weapons tiers, fishing and taming, the Fire Offerings merchant, and the [Forest Rage hard mode]({{< relref "/advanced/forest-rage-hard-mode" >}}) prep checklist for anyone who has beaten 99 nights.
- [Videos]({{< relref "/media" >}}) — 60-plus community walkthroughs sorted by quest, boss, biome and taming, so you can watch the rescue route before you attempt it.
