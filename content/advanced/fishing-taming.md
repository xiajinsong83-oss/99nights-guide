Fishing is the most famous side activity in 99 Nights in the Forest, and fame is weird: nobody would care about fishing in a survival game if it did not hide the best-kept chat code in the game. Taming is the quieter system underneath it — the animal companions that end up doing real work around your camp.

## Fishing, from the beginning

Fishing is locked behind a fire upgrade, which is the part everyone misses:

1. **Upgrade the campfire to level 2.** This unlocks the fishing rod route. No level 2, no rod, no code.
2. **Build the fishing rod.** It comes from the unlocked route once the fire is up.
3. **Find a fishing spot** and cast the rod so it sits in the water. Standing on the bank is not enough.
4. **Reel in the bite.** Catches land as food, with some catches converting into useful materials.

## The yay fishing chat code

With the rod in the water at a fishing spot, press **/** to open chat and type exactly:

`yay fishing`

Two Diamonds spawn instantly. Three conditions, all required: the campfire at level 2, the rod actually in the water, and the literal space between the words. No level 2 fire and the code refuses to exist in your game. We link to the full redemption ritual on the [Codes page]({{< relref "/codes/redeem" >}}), but the short version is this: if `yay fishing` ever "does not work," your fire is level 1.

## What fishing is actually good for

- **Renewable food.** A fishing spot by camp means your food buffer never runs dry mid-siege. The August 8 Kids Have Jobs update even added a fish-catching job for Kraken Kid, so fishing now pays into the daycare loop as well.
- **Free Diamonds.** The chat code is small, but free is free.
- **Materials.** Certain catches convert into crafting resources, which softens the gathering grind.

## Taming: how a pet actually joins you

Taming takes the right tool and the right food. The documented recipe for taming a bear, the most useful companion in the game, is:

- **Tamer's Flute upgraded to level 3**, plus a bait pile of **5 salmon, 5 steak and 5 pumpkin**. Bring all three to the bear, work through the interaction, and it comes home with you.

Tamed animals then live at your base and provide their role bonus. The community has a full timelined video covering the tame recipe for every animal added in the Taming Update, so for anything beyond the bear the recipe list is worth pulling up.

## Why wolves are the one to skip

The one taming takeaway that saves you the most wasted food: **do not tame wolves**. The community verdict after testing is blunt — wolf companions fail at survival value and do not pull their weight around camp. Tame the bear, tame the useful gatherers, and leave the wolves as wildlife.

## Fishing as the food engine

The real value of fishing is not the 2-Diamond code, it is that a fishing spot near camp turns your food buffer from a chore into a renewable resource. The August 8 Kids Have Jobs update doubled down on this: Kraken Kid now runs a fish-catching job, so a day spent at the bank feeds both the squad and the daycare loop. For long runs, that renewable food source is what lets the Cook class stop being the bottleneck.

## Field tips

- Fish in **daytime**. Night fishing pulls threats to the bank, and the Deer does not care that you just landed a nice salmon.
- Keep a spare rod in base storage so any squad member can fish without crafting a new one.
- Tamed animals that gather save a full player's worth of daily work, which is the whole point: they are extra hands, not decoration.
- Prioritize the bear tame early. It is the companion that pays for itself fastest, and the 5-salmon / 5-steak / 5-pumpkin bait pile is cheap compared with a bear that fights for your base at night.

## Why the bear is the tame to book first

Of all the animals you can befriend, the bear is the one that earns its keep fastest. It fights for the base at night and the bait pile is cheap next to the value of a companion that does real defense work. Wolves, by contrast, have been tested and left wanting — do not spend salmon on them.

The level 3 Tamer's Flute is the gate, so plan the taming route around upgrading the flute rather than hoarding bait. The bear tame pays for the flute upgrade within a few nights of defense work.
