Forest Rage is the hard-mode layer of 99 Nights in the Forest — reached after the main run, it raises threat scaling and adds the Corruption mechanic, Rift clearing and the Research Outpost end-game objective.

## What changes in Forest Rage

- **Threat scaling** — monsters hit harder, spawn earlier and scale faster.
- **Corruption** — corrupted zones spread across the map and must be managed.
- **Rifts** — clearing Rifts is the way to push back Corruption.
- **Research Outpost** — the late-game objective that ties the hard-mode loop together.

## How to prepare

1. **Max the campfire and bench** before entering — hard mode punishes weak cores.
2. **Stockpile wood and food** — double what a normal run needs.
3. **Collect Modifier Flames early** — [Flames](/advanced/modifier-flames/) are the real damage multiplier in hard mode.
4. **Squad up** — hard mode is brutal solo.

## The hard-mode loop (framework)

1. **Day**: clear Corruption at the edges, gather, upgrade.
2. **Dusk**: close the base — hard-mode nights attack harder and smarter.
3. **Rift windows**: when a Rift is active, a strike squad clears it to reduce Corruption pressure.
4. **Outpost**: repair and research at the Research Outpost to progress the final objective.
