Modifier Flames are upgrade items that attach to your weapons and tools to change how they behave. They're one of the main reasons late-game loadouts feel completely different from early-game ones.

## What Flames do

- **Modify equipment** — attach to a weapon/tool to change its properties (damage type, effects, resource yield).
- **Stack with tiers** — a good Flame on an upgraded weapon beats a plain top-tier weapon.
- **Are random** — code rewards like **forestwakesup26** grant *random* Flames, so collection is part of the loop.

## How to get Flames

- **Code rewards** — e.g. the current working code gives **3 random Flames** (see [Codes](/codes/)).
- **Gameplay drops** — late-night kills and event content drop Flames.

## How to apply them

1. Open your inventory/equipment menu.
2. Select the weapon or tool you want to modify.
3. Attach the Flame (check the compatibility note — some Flames only fit certain item types).
4. Confirm — the item's stats update immediately.

## Quick tips

- **Don't waste good Flames on starter tools** — wait until mid-tier at least.
- **Match Flame type to role** — combat classes want damage Flames; Lumberjack/Scavenger prefer resource-yield Flames.
- **Flames are often the difference** in [Forest Rage hard mode](/advanced/forest-rage-hard-mode/) — start collecting early.

{{< notice title="Verify in-game" >}}Flame names, effects and compatibility are balance-sensitive — confirm the current behavior in-game before planning builds around a specific Flame.{{< /notice >}}
