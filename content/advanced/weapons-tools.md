Your crafting bench is the whole economy of 99 Nights in the Forest. Everything you survive with — tools, weapons, armor, map and compass — comes out of it, and its upgrade level gates what you can build.

## Crafting bench tiers (framework)

The bench upgrades unlock recipe groups in order. The exact item names and material costs shift with updates — this is the progression shape, not a fixed price table.

| Bench tier | Unlocks (typical) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Basic axe, spear, simple storage |
| 2 | Better tools, traps, shelves |
| 3 | Weapons tier-up, armor basics |
| 4+ | Late-game weapons, advanced traps, compass/map tools |

{{< notice title="Verify in-game" >}}Always confirm current recipe names and material costs at your bench — balance patches re-tune these regularly.{{< /notice >}}

## Core tools

- **Axe** — wood gathering; upgrading it is the single biggest economy multiplier.
- **Spear** — the reliable early weapon; cheap, ranged, safe from inside walls.
- **Map & Compass** — unlock the exploration loop and rescue hunting; vital for late game.

## Weapons by phase

| Phase | Best weapon | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Spear | Safe from behind walls |
| Mid | Upgraded spear/axe | Damage catches up to threats |
| Late | Top-tier weapons from bench | Bear and hard-mode fights demand burst |

## Loadout recommendations

- **Defense night**: spear + repair stack + light.
- **Gathering day**: axe + food + no heavy armor.
- **Rescue run**: weapons + map/compass + light + partner.

> Defense loadouts pair with [Base Building]({{< relref "/guide/base-building" >}}); damage builds pair with [Modifier Flames]({{< relref "/advanced/modifier-flames" >}}).
