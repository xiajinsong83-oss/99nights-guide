## What the advanced layer actually changes

The early game is a gather-build-defend loop. The mid game is where the systems underneath it wake up: your crafting bench tier decides which weapons you can build, your campfire level 3 opens the caves, Flames turn plain gear into specialized tools, and the Sketchy Salesman starts drifting through your camp at night selling Fire Offerings. Hard mode — Forest Rage — is the endgame layer where Corruption, Rifts and the Research Outpost take over.

- **[Forest Rage Hard Mode]({{< relref "/advanced/forest-rage-hard-mode" >}})** — the full prep checklist: what Corruption does, how Rifts get closed, and why the Research Outpost is the objective the whole hard-mode loop exists around.

## Who should read these now

Players sitting around night 20–30 wondering why their gear feels weak, players chasing the Diamond economy from code rewards, and anyone staring down the choice between more base work and stepping into hard mode. The common thread: advanced systems stop being about surviving the night and start being about *how fast you can scale*.

Do not open hard mode on a whim. The prep checklist exists because flipping the lever under-prepared is the most expensive mistake in the late game. Read the four pages in order, bank your Flames, and only then think about the Research Outpost.

The common thread across these pages is scaling. Once the first ten nights feel routine, the question stops being "can I survive tonight" and becomes "how fast can I build the gear and the economy to face what comes after night 99." Weapons tiers, Flames, fishing and taming, and the Corruption/Rift loop all feed that scaling. Skim them in order before you commit to a late-game push.
