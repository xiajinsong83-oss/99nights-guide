[[faq]]
  q = "Is 99 Nights in the Forest free to play?"
  a = "Yes. It is a free Roblox game, and the whole gather-build-defend loop plus the rescue quests are free. Some classes and cosmetics cost Gems or Robux, but none of the core progression is paywalled."

[[faq]]
  q = "What platforms can I play it on?"
  a = "Wherever Roblox runs: PC, Mac, iOS, Android and Xbox. Your progress is tied to your Roblox account, not the device."

[[faq]]
  q = "How many players can play together?"
  a = "It is built around squads of 2 to 4 players, and that is the sweet spot. Pack attacks and the Deer punish solo wanderers, while extra hands split chopping, building and night watch."

[[faq]]
  q = "Can I play solo?"
  a = "You can, but it is a harder game. Solo players should extend the early phase, never wander at night, and rely on traps and kiting instead of fair fights. Push the campfire to level 2 early; the bigger light radius is the solo player's best friend."

[[faq]]
  q = "How do I play with friends?"
  a = "Join the same Roblox server: open your friends list and join a friend's game, or share a server invite. Once everyone is in, build the camp together."

[[faq]]
  q = "Is 99 Nights in the Forest based on a movie?"
  a = "The game has a 20th Century Studios movie tie-in that brought a lot of new players in. The game itself is a co-op survival experience on Roblox made by Grandma's Favourite Games."

[[faq]]
  q = "How do I rescue the missing children?"
  a = "The bulletin board starts the quest. Follow the red-arrow markers across the map, each rescue ends in a guardian fight, then you carry the child back to camp. Rescuing all four plus expanding the bonfire is the route to skipping roughly 8 days of progression per night."

[[faq]]
  q = "Are the codes case-sensitive?"
  a = "No. Spelling has to be exact, including the space inside 'yay fishing', but upper and lower case do not matter."

[[faq]]
  q = "Why does yay fishing not work for me?"
  a = "Three conditions: campfire level 2, the fishing rod actually in the water at a fishing spot, and the space between the words. Skip any one and the 2 Diamonds never arrive. If your fire is level 1, the code does not exist in your game yet."

[[faq]]
  q = "How many working codes are there right now?"
  a = "Four as of our October 3 verification: awardwinner_yay, forestwakesup26 and afterparty in the lobby, plus yay fishing as the in-chat code. They pay out 47 free Diamonds in total. The live list is on the [Codes page](/codes)."

[[faq]]
  q = "What should I upgrade first — campfire or crafting bench?"
  a = "Campfire to level 2 first, then crafting bench to level 2, then campfire to level 3. The level 2 fire unlocks the fishing route and a safer light radius; the bench multiplies every future gathering run. Full reasoning on the [Campfire Upgrades page](/guide/campfire-upgrades)."

[[faq]]
  q = "Which class should a new player pick?"
  a = "Start as the free Camper to learn the loop, then buy Medic or Lumberjack. Medic's heals and revives decide more night outcomes than any weapon, and Lumberjack runs the whole wood economy."

[[faq]]
  q = "What breaks my walls?"
  a = "The Ram. It charges straight through wooden fences. Keep a repair stack of wood inside the base and watch the lane it picks, because it picks one and commits."

[[faq]]
  q = "Can I tame animals?"
  a = "Yes. A level 3 Tamer's Flute plus 5 salmon, 5 steak and 5 pumpkin tames a bear, the most useful companion. Skip wolves — community testing found they do not pull their weight around camp."

[[faq]]
  q = "What happens when I die?"
  a = "You lose the materials you were carrying. Travel light, bring haul home before dark, and do not scout far with a pack full of resources."

[[faq]]
  q = "What is Forest Rage hard mode?"
  a = "The endgame layer reached after the main run: Corruption spreads across the map, Rifts open that you have to deactivate, and the Research Outpost becomes the objective. The full prep checklist is on the [hard mode page](/advanced/forest-rage-hard-mode)."

We keep this list to the things players actually ask in lobbies and in our inbox, not to generic survival-game filler: which upgrades come first, why the chat code refuses to pay out, what the missing-children quest actually is, and how Forest Rage changes the rules.

A few framing notes before you scroll. First, codes in this game do not run on a schedule — they attach to patches, so a code list that has not been refreshed since summer will show dead strings as live. Our [Codes page]({{< relref "/codes" >}}) shows the verification date on purpose, and the expired table exists so you can tell a working code from a fake one. Second, the early game has one big unlock that nobody tells you about: campfire level 2 opens the fishing route, and the fishing route is what makes `yay fishing` real. Most new players type that code in the lobby chat, get nothing, and decide the list is wrong. It is not.

One more thing before you dive in: the September 2026 updates moved a lot of rules. The Class Shop now sells every class permanently, three new classes joined the roster, and several biome rewards relocated during the August refreshes. If a tip you remember from an older video does not seem to work in-game, check the date on that advice before blaming your setup.
