[[faq]]
  q = "Is 99 Nights in the Forest free to play?"
  a = "Yes. It's a free Roblox game. Some classes and cosmetics cost Gems or Robux, but the core game and all progression are free."

[[faq]]
  q = "What platform can I play it on?"
  a = "It runs wherever Roblox runs: PC, Mac, iOS, Android, Xbox and VR. Progress is tied to your Roblox account."

[[faq]]
  q = "How many players can play together?"
  a = "It's a co-op game designed for squads. Server capacity varies, but the game is built around groups of 2-4+ players working together."

[[faq]]
  q = "Can I play 99 Nights in the Forest solo?"
  a = "You can, but it's significantly harder. Solo players should extend the early 'core' phase, never wander at night, and rely on traps and kiting rather than direct fights."

[[faq]]
  q = "How do I play with friends?"
  a = "Join the same Roblox server: join a friend's game from the Roblox friends list, or use a server code/join link if the game provides one. Then build your camp together."

[[faq]]
  q = "Is 99 Nights in the Forest based on a movie?"
  a = "The game has a 20th Century Studios movie tie-in that has drawn in many new players. The game itself is a co-op survival horror experience on Roblox."

[[faq]]
  q = "How do I rescue the missing children?"
  a = "Explore systematically with food, weapons and light. Mark clues across the map; finding and following clues unlocks rescue runs and late-game content."

[[faq]]
  q = "Are the codes case-sensitive?"
  a = "No. Codes can be entered in any combination of upper and lower case as long as the spelling is correct — including the space in 'yay fishing'."

[[faq]]
