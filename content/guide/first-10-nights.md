This is the route most new squads follow to get through the first 10 nights without wiping. Build by day, guard the fire by night.

## Days 1–2 · Get established

- Spend daylight **chopping wood** and gathering food to keep hunger up.
- Craft a **basic axe** and **spear**.
- At night: stay glued to the campfire. Never overextend in the dark.

## Days 3–4 · Scale up

- Once wood is stockpiled, push **crafting bench upgrades first** — they unlock better tools and recipes.
- Faster tools compound every resource run afterwards.

## Days 5–6 · Core priority

- The **campfire** is your lifeline — level it whenever possible.
- **Level 2 campfire also unlocks the fishing chat code** (yay fishing → 2 Gems).
- Build **shelves and crates**, create a **food buffer** so nobody starves mid-siege.

## Days 7–8 · Defenses

- Night threats ramp up here.
- Ring the campfire with **walls**, line attack paths with **traps**.
- **Split the night watch** — one player guards while the other restocks.

## Days 9–10 · Expand

- With the base stable, explore **systematically** — food, weapons and light.
- Mark clues toward the **missing children** to set up rescue runs and late-game content.

## Route summary

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Establish | 1–2 | Wood, food, basic tools |
| Scale | 3–4 | Crafting bench upgrades |
| Core | 5–6 | Campfire levels, food buffer, fishing unlock |
| Defend | 7–8 | Walls, traps, watch rotation |
| Expand | 9–10 | Exploration, rescue prep |

> The exact day thresholds shift with squad size — bigger groups can push phases faster; solo players should extend the "Core" phase by a day or two.
