The first 10 nights are where new squads die, and they die in a recognizable pattern: day one is exciting, night two teaches respect, and night four or five the whole base gets walked over because nobody upgraded the fire. This route is the version of those 10 nights we have re-run until the squad stops losing members. It is a checklist, not a lecture.

## Days 1–2: get established

- Spend every daylight minute **chopping wood** and picking up food. Hunger is your first silent killer, not the Deer.
- Build the **crafting bench**, then craft a **basic axe** and a **spear**. The spear stays relevant until late game, because it lets you hit enemies from behind walls.
- At night: stand in the light. No scouting, no chasing, no hero plays. If something bumps the wall, poke it from inside the glow.

What a good end-of-day-2 looks like: bench built, axe in hand, a small wood pile, and nobody scarred.

## Days 3–4: scale the economy

- Once the wood pile exists, **push crafting bench upgrades first**. The bench unlocks better tools and recipes, and better tools make every later gathering run faster. This compounds; campfire levels do not, which is why the bench comes first.
- Use the improved tools to stock food. We like a food buffer of several meals per player before night 5, because the raids arriving around then love nothing more than a hungry squad.

## Days 5–6: the campfire phase

This is the phase most routes skip and everyone regrets.

- **Push the campfire to level 2 as a team priority.** The bigger light radius is what makes nights 5–6 survivable, and level 2 also unlocks the **fishing rod route** — which is how the `yay fishing` chat code starts paying 2 free Diamonds. You can absolutely go six nights without realizing this unlock exists.
- Build **shelves and crates** and separate the food storage from the upgrade-material storage. Builders strip the food pile when everything shares one crate.
- Start scouting near landmarks in daylight. Mark where the bulletin board sits, because the rescue chain is about to matter.

## Days 7–8: walls and watch rotations

- Night threats ramp hard around here. Ring the campfire with a **wall ring** and lay **traps on the paths enemies actually use** — not evenly, on the lanes.
- Split the **night watch**: one player guards the walls while another rests, repairs and restocks. Everyone fighting at once is how an overrun happens.
- Keep a **repair stack of wood** accessible from inside the base. The Ram shows up around this phase and charges straight through fences. A repair crate beats a fresh wall every time.

## Days 9–10: begin the rescue engine

- With the base holding on its own, turn to the **missing children**. The bulletin board starts the quest chain, red arrows point you across the map, and each rescue ends in a guardian fight before you carry the child home.
- Why bother now? Because rescuing all four children plus expanding the bonfire is the known route to skipping roughly 8 days of progression per night. Starting it on day 9 means your mid-game begins on a completely different curve than squads who wait until night 30 to notice the board.

## Route summary

| Phase | Days | The one thing that matters |
|---|---|---|
| Establish | 1–2 | Bench, axe, spear, nobody wandering at night |
| Scale | 3–4 | Bench upgrades, food buffer |
| Core | 5–6 | Campfire level 2, storage separation, fishing unlock |
| Defend | 7–8 | Wall ring, traps on lanes, repair crate, watch rotation |
| Expand | 9–10 | Missing-children rescue chain starts |

## Why squads wipe around night 4–5

When a first run dies, it almost always dies here, and the reasons are predictable:

- **Nobody owned the wood pile.** With no dedicated chopper, the bench never upgrades and the campfire stays level 1 while the spawns scale.
- **The food buffer never got built.** A siege at night 4 with two meals total is a siege you lose to hunger, not to damage.
- **The fire stayed level 1.** The tiny light radius means anyone leaning out for wood gets picked off by the Deer. This is the unlock the whole route exists to fix.

If your squad has died twice in a row by night 5, do not change the plan — check which of those three got skipped.

## Adjust this for your squad size

- **Full squad of 3–4** can compress each phase by about a day.
- **Solo** should extend the Core phase by a day or two and skip the early rescue sprint. A solo player kites the Deer through traps rather than fighting, so the campfire level 2 radius matters more, not less.
