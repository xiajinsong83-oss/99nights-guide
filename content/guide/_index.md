This is the section most of you will open first, so let us save you the scrolling. The guides below are ordered the way we actually hand them to a friend joining the game after the movie tie-in brought them in. Do not read them backwards: every page assumes the lessons of the earlier ones.

## Read order

1. **[How to Play]({{< relref "/guide/how-to-play" >}})** — the loop. Day is for gathering and building, night is for keeping the campfire alive. If you have never survived a full night, read this one before you touch anything else. It also explains what the real objectives are, because "survive 99 nights" sounds like the goal and it is not, not at the start.
2. **[First 10 Nights Route]({{< relref "/guide/first-10-nights" >}})** — the day-by-day checklist. This is the page we send to every new squad. It tells you when to push the crafting bench, when to hit campfire level 2, and when to stop exploring and wall up. The first 10 nights are where 80% of runs die, and they die the same four ways every time.
3. **[Campfire Upgrades]({{< relref "/guide/campfire-upgrades" >}})** — what each fire level actually changes, including the level 2 fishing unlock that most players miss for weeks, and the order we recommend between fire levels and the crafting bench.
4. **[Base Building]({{< relref "/guide/base-building" >}})** — walls, traps, storage and the night-watch rotation. Read this the first time the Ram demolishes your fence, which will be sooner than you think.

## The mistakes we watch new squads make

- **Wandering at night to grab one more tree.** The Deer circles your fire waiting for exactly this. If you can see the dark beyond the light radius, you are too far.
- **Hoarding wood instead of upgrading the bench.** A pile of logs in a crate buys nothing. Better tools multiply every future gathering run, which is why bench level 2 comes before big stockpiles.
- **Forgetting fuel.** A stacked base with an empty fire dies the first night the cultists raid. Keep a fuel crate by the fire and check it at dusk.
- **Splitting up after night 5.** Pack attacks and the Deer punish solo roamers. Two players together clear every early threat cheaper than one hero does.

## Start where your run actually is

If your base has never held a full night, you need the loop and the route. If it holds but your gear feels weak by night 20, you need the bench and weapons pages. If you are staring down hard mode, stop reading the basics and go to [Forest Rage]({{< relref "/advanced/forest-rage-hard-mode" >}}). The mistake is reading everything out of order and forgetting to apply the page that matches your current night.

## What these guides assume about you

We write for a 2–4 player squad on a fresh server, playing the normal mode. Solo players get notes where the math changes — solo needs an extra day or two in the early phase and leans on traps and kiting, never on fair fights. Hard mode is a separate problem set on its own [Advanced page]({{< relref "/advanced/forest-rage-hard-mode" >}}); do not try to apply early-game advice inside Forest Rage.

Numbers in these pages are strategy, not a price list. Wood costs and recipe unlocks shift with patches — the September 2026 Class Shop Revamp moved a pile of economy values — so confirm the current cost in-game before a big spend.
