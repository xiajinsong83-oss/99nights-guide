A good base in 99 Nights in the Forest is a ring of defenses around a strong campfire, with storage inside and choke points outside.

## The standard layout

1. **Campfire core** — everything is built within its light radius.
2. **Wall ring** — one layer early, two layers once the Ram starts appearing.
3. **Trap lanes** — traps on the paths enemies use, not spread evenly.
4. **Storage inside** — shelves and crates next to the fire, so defenders restock fast.

## Walls: the basics

- Walls stop the **Deer's minions** and **Cultists**, but **Ram charges through** — keep a repair stack of wood.
- Reinforce **weak fence sections before** the Ram picks a lane.
- Gaps are fine for shooting; open lanes are not.

## Traps

- Line **attack paths** with traps rather than walling every meter.
- Re-arm traps between nights — a spent trap is a hole in your defense.
- Later traps (once unlocked at the bench) can handle the Bear; early traps mostly stall.

## Shelves & crates

- Shelves and crates are **cheap storage** that keeps food and materials safe and close.
- A **food buffer** of several meals per player prevents starvation mid-siege.
- Keep upgrade materials (wood) in a **separate crate** so builders don't strip the food storage.

## Night-watch rotation

- **One player guards while the other restocks** — both moving at night is how bases get overrun.
- Assign lanes before dusk: east/west or wall/trap duty.
- Call out Ram charges early so the builder can react.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Building too far from the fire | Keep everything inside the light radius |
| One big perimeter | Multiple small rings beat one huge ring |
| No repair stock | Keep wood crate accessible from inside |
| All players fighting | One watches the fire at all times |
