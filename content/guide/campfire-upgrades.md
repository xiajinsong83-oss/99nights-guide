The campfire is the most upgraded thing on any base and the most misunderstood. It glows in the middle of camp, so everyone assumes it is a decoration you keep topped up. It is not. The fire is the progression gate of the game: its light radius defines where you are allowed to fight, its level gates the fishing route and the cave, and its survival through the night is the only thing that actually advances your run. Build walls before the fire and you get a pretty ring around a weak heart.

## What the fire does at each stage

- **Level 1.** Tiny light radius. The Deer can circle just outside the glow and pick off anyone who leans two tiles too far for wood. Walls at level 1 mostly protect the fire, not the squad.
- **Level 2.** The radius jumps enough to kite inside the light instead of getting dragged into the dark. This is the stage where nights 5–6 stop being a coin flip. It also unlocks the **fishing rod route**, and the fishing route is what makes the `yay fishing` chat code work — the code only pays out while a rod sits in the water. Two free Diamonds, but more importantly it proves your fishing setup is live.
- **Level 3.** Unlocks the **cave** entrance (the community runbooks call for a flashlight, food and a rifle before you go in). This is where the cave biome, the bat enemies and the statue blessings open up.
- **Level 4 and beyond.** Bigger radius and stronger anchor for the late game, but expensive. At this point your walls and traps should already be holding, so the wood is buying comfort, not survival.

## The order we recommend

After re-running the early phase a few times, this sequence produced the least-dead squad:

1. **Campfire to level 2 before bench level 3.** Get the fire up by night 3 if one player chops exclusively for the first two days. The light radius plus the fishing unlock buys you the exact window where most first runs die.
2. **Bench to level 2, then fire to level 3.** The bench gives you the tools that make chopping faster, and faster chopping funds the fire. Tools compound; fire levels do not, which is why the bench takes this slot.
3. **Park the fire at level 3 until the walls hold.** The top fire levels cost heavy wood that is better spent on weapons and traps while you are still in the night-10-to-30 stretch.
4. **Level 4+ only when you are preparing for hard mode.** Once Forest Rage is on the table, the bigger radius and the stronger anchor matter again.

One honest caveat: exact wood and material costs per level move with balance patches — the September 2026 Class Shop Revamp touched a lot of economy numbers — so check the live cost in-game before committing a big spend. The order above is strategy; the prices are whatever the current patch says.

## The habits that make the fire work

- **Assign a fire-keeper.** In a four-player squad, one person owns the fire: they track its level, chop specifically for it, and upgrade the moment the wood exists. Without an owner, upgrades get forgotten mid-day and you discover it at dusk with an empty pile.
- **Fuel check before dusk.** A base with full walls and no fuel loses the second the fire dies mid-raid. The fire does not care how pretty your ring is.
- **The fire and the bench are partners, not rivals.** The common mistake is treating them as competing spending. They are a pipeline: bench tools make chopping faster, faster chopping funds fire levels. The only real choice is timing, which is what the order above is for.

## Why level 3 is the unlock people sleep on

Campfire level 3 is the gate nobody mentions in the quick tips: it opens the **cave** system. The community runbooks for the cave bring a flashlight, food and a rifle, because underground is where the bat enemies and the statue blessings live. That is the real reason the order parks the fire at level 3 — not because level 4 is bad, but because the wood between 3 and 4 buys you weapons and traps while the cave content is still unexplored.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why it hurts | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| Maxing the fire before the bench | Radius you cannot exploit with weak tools | Bench level 2 first, then fire |
| Buying level 4 before hard mode | Wood that should be weapons and traps | Park the fire at 3 |
| Expecting `yay fishing` to work at level 1 | The fishing route needs level 2 | Level 2, build the rod, then chat-code |
| No fuel reserve at dusk | One raid and the fire dies | Fuel crate by the fire, restocked daily |
