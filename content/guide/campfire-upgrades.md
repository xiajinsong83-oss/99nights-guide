The campfire is your light, heat and anchor. Upgrading it changes how safely your squad can play — and it gates content like the fishing chat code.

## Why the campfire matters

- **Night survival** — a brighter, higher-level fire keeps threats at bay and marks your safe zone.
- **Content unlocks** — level 2 grants access to the **fishing rod** path, which enables the **yay fishing** chat code (2 Gems).
- **Base value** — the fire area is where walls, traps, shelves and crates do their work; a weak fire makes a strong base pointless.

## Upgrade priorities (framework order)

1. **Campfire to level 2 as soon as possible** — fishing unlock and a much safer nights 5–6 window.
2. **Level 3+ before expanding the base** — bigger defenses only pay off if the core is stable.
3. **Leave the very last levels for late-game** — they cost heavy wood and materials that are better spent on weapons first.

{{< notice >}}Exact wood/material costs per campfire level change with balance patches — always verify the live numbers in-game before planning a big spend. This is the intended priority order, not a fixed price table.{{< /notice >}}

## Quick tips

- Assign a **dedicated fire-keeper** in squads so upgrades aren't forgotten mid-day.
- Stock **fire fuel / wood** before dusk — running out at night is a run-ender.
- The campfire upgrade path pairs with the [Crafting Bench upgrades](/guide/base-building/) — do both, bench first.
