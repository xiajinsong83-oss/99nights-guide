99 Nights in the Forest is a **co-op survival horror game on Roblox**: by day you gather, craft and build; by night, creatures attack your campfire. Your run succeeds when you survive the nights, keep the fire burning, and rescue the missing children.

## The core loop

Every day follows the same rhythm:

1. **Gather** — chop wood, pick up food and materials around the map.
2. **Craft & upgrade** — the crafting bench unlocks better tools; upgrades cost wood and materials.
3. **Prepare** — upgrade the campfire, build walls and traps, stock food.
4. **Defend** — survive the night. Keep the campfire bright; darkness is death.
5. **Repeat** — each night is harder, and later nights push you further from base.

## Your real objectives

- **Protect the campfire** — it's your light, your heat and your lifeline. If it goes out, the night goes very badly.
- **Rescue the missing children** — exploration and clue-hunting across the map lead to rescues and the late-game content.
- **Survive 99 nights** — the long game. Early nights are for learning; later nights (and Forest Rage hard mode) are the real test.

## Survival basics for new players

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Never wander at night without light | The Deer and other threats hunt in darkness |
| Keep food stockpiled | Hunger kills runs mid-siege |
| Upgrade the bench before hoarding | Better tools multiply every resource run |
| Stay together after night 5 | Pack attacks punish solo wanderers |
| Walls ≠ invincible | Ram breaks walls; keep a repair stack of wood |

## What to do on your very first day

Chop wood until you have a basic axe and spear. Stay near the campfire at night. Don't overextend — dying loses your carried materials.
