The Deer is the signature threat of 99 Nights in the Forest — it circles your campfire and punishes anyone who wanders into the dark. You don't need to out-fight it early; you need to out-position it.

## How The Deer behaves

- **Circles the fire** — it patrols the perimeter of your light, waiting for a gap.
- **Attacks darkness** — anyone outside the campfire radius becomes its priority target.
- **Minions** — it directs Cultists and can be accompanied by them on later nights.
- **Patience over aggression** — it rarely rushes a well-lit, walled camp head-on.

## The safe counter strategy

1. **Never leave the light** — the campfire radius is your safe zone; all fights happen inside it.
2. **Keep walls intact** — a single gap becomes its entrance. Repair during daylight.
3. **Fight it in light, with a partner** — one player draws it to the fire edge, the other damages it.
4. **Use traps on its patrol lane** — the lane it circles is predictable; line it with traps.
5. **Don't chase it into the dark** — chasing is how solo players die.

## Gear you want

| Stage | Recommended |
|---|---|
| Early (nights 1–6) | Spear + walls, kite it |
| Mid (nights 7–12) | Upgraded spear/axe, traps, 2 players |
| Late | Best melee/weapons from the bench, full squad |

## Squad vs solo

- **Squad**: one baiter at the fire edge + one striker behind. Rotate so nobody tanks twice in a row.
- **Solo**: never engage head-on. Kite it through trap lanes and repair walls after each pass.

## Common deaths — and fixes

| Death | Fix |
|---|---|
| Wandered out of light | Set a personal "don't cross" marker at dusk |
| Chased it | Let it come to you |
| Wall gap | Repair check every morning |
| Ran out of food mid-siege | Food buffer in a crate by the fire |
