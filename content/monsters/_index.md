## Counter summaries

### The Deer
The main predator circling your fire. Keep the campfire bright and walls intact, and never wander off alone at night. Deep dive: [How to Beat The Deer](/monsters/deer/).

### Cultists
Attack the camp in groups on a timer. Fight them through traps and fortifications — trading blows in the open gets expensive.

### The Owl
Walls won't stop it. It messes with your light sources — stay in bright zones and keep looking up during watch.

### Ram
Charges straight at wooden defenses. Pre-reinforce weak fence sections before it picks a lane.

### Wolves
Dangerous away from the fire and lethal to solo explorers. Travel in pairs and fight them one at a time.

### Bear
Do not engage early. With weak gear, use terrain and traps to kite it; a full squad with top weapons is the minimum for a fair fight.

### Bats
Low individual threat but they swarm cave biomes at night. Bring a light source and don't corner yourself underground.
