Half of surviving 99 Nights is knowing what is walking toward your base. The game does not tell you which night the Ram arrives, why the Deer is suddenly out in daylight, or that cultists actually run on a rough 3–4 day raid timer. This page is the enemy compendium: what each threat does, when it shows up, and the counter that costs the least wood.

## The threat table

| Threat | How dangerous | What it actually does |
|---|---|---|
| The Deer | Extreme | Signature night stalker, circles your fire, picks off anyone in the dark |
| Cultists | High | Raid the camp in groups on a 3–4 day cycle |
| Cultist King | Boss | Spawns through the raid chain; ranged kiting boss |
| Bear / Polar Bear | High | Apex, punishes weak gear fast |
| Alpha Wolf | High | Pack leader, lethal to solo explorers |
| Ram | High | Charger that demolishes wooden walls |
| Wolves | Medium | Pack hunters, dangerous away from the fire |
| Owl | Medium | Messes with your light sources |
| Bats | Low | Cave swarms, weak alone, many underground |
| Snow biome animals | Medium | Freeze-debuff pressure on exposed players |

## The short version of each fight

- **The Deer** does not want a fair fight. It circles the light radius and waits for a gap. Community testing found it has a hidden scaling mechanic tied to your progress, which is why a late-run Deer feels completely different from an early one. It also directs cultists on heavier nights. The full breakdown lives on the [Deer page]({{< relref "/monsters/deer" >}}).
- **Cultists raid on a timer.** Not every night, roughly every 3–4 days, in growing groups. Walls funnel them into traps, and the community's kill-funnel maze base design survives 500-plus days farming exactly these raids.
- **The Ram** is a wall problem, not a combat problem. It charges straight through fences. Keep repair wood inside the ring and watch the lane it picks.
- **Wolves** hunt in packs and shred solo explorers. Travel in pairs after night 5.
- **Bears** are the early "do not engage" threat. Community rifle testing counts the shots needed per animal, and the takeaway for new squads is blunt: a weak rifle eats most of a magazine on a wolf, so bears stay a kiting and traps problem until your weapons catch up.
- **Bats** live in the caves you unlock after campfire level 3. Bring a flashlight, food and a rifle, and never corner yourself underground.

## When each one shows up

Early rule of thumb: the Deer and small cultist pressure arrive from the first nights, wolves and the wall-breaking Ram show up in the mid stretch around night 5 onward, bears stay a late-game threat, and the owl plus snow and cave enemies open up as you explore their biomes. Nighttime spawns scale with how many nights you have survived, which means a base that held at night 10 can fail at night 30 for the exact same layout.

{{< notice title="Verify in-game" >}}Spawn patterns and balance shift with updates and difficulty settings. Treat the ratings as our read of the current patch, not as a fixed timetable.{{< /notice >}}
