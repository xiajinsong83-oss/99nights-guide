Some things in this game you have to see before you run them. Where the missing-children bulletin board sends you, how the Cultist King kites, what a kill-funnel maze base actually looks like on night 200 — text describes these, but watching one clear run saves a wiped squad. This archive collects community walkthroughs and runs for 99 Nights in the Forest, sorted by category so you can pull the exact video you need instead of scrolling YouTube.

## How the archive is organized

- **Quest Walkthrough** — the big scripted routes: all four missing children, the four Crystal Skull Keys and the Jungle Temple secret, the 8-days-per-night skip method, and the four volcano biome missions.
- **Survival Guide** — long-run strategy: surviving hundreds of days, the broken cultist strategy, the AFK base with Crock Pot stew automation that survived a 200-day test, and the tree-and-sapling hide spot for cultist raids.
- **Boss Guide** — the Deer's secret scaling mechanic, why you should stop running, the Cultist King guide, the triple-wall fortress test, the hurt-Deer mystery and the Ram update.
- **Codes** — working-code demos, the yay fishing secret, and the fake-code warning you need before trusting random lists.
- **Update Coverage** — hard-mode activation, the caves and bat enemy, the volcanic update, and what changed in recent patches.
- **Biome Guide** — snow, winter, fairy and volcanic biome deep dives, plus the Ice Bow showcase.
- **Events & Badges** — hidden Easter egg locations, the secret Easter Bunny Egg, and the Bloxfest badge routes.
- **Taming & Pets** — taming a bear with the level 3 flute, every animal's tame recipe, and why wolves are not worth taming.
- **Lore & Fan** — the story of the Deer, the alien-frog lore thread, and fan music.

## How to use videos with the written guides

We read them in order: pick the written page for the *what and why*, then pull the matching video for the *how it looks in motion*. Before a rescue run, skim [First 10 Nights]({{< relref "/guide/first-10-nights" >}}), then open the missing-kids walkthrough so the guardian fights are not a surprise. Before hard mode, read the [Forest Rage prep checklist]({{< relref "/advanced/forest-rage-hard-mode" >}}) and watch the 10-tips hard-mode run alongside it.

The videos are sorted by what you are trying to survive, not by upload date. Quest walkthroughs live with the rescue and temple routes, boss guides with the Deer and cultist fights, biome guides with snow, fairy and volcanic deep dives, and taming demos with the pet recipes. Pick the category that matches your current problem and you will not have to scroll through a casual gameplay clip to find the one useful run.

The videos pair with the written guides rather than replacing them: read the page for the strategy, then watch the run to see how the fights and routes actually look in motion. Start with the category that matches the night you are about to enter.
