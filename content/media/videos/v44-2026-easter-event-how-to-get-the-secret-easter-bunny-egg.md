## What this video actually shows

The secret Easter Bunny Egg is the event's hidden prestige item — you do not find it by sweeping the map, you take it from behind the Evil Bunny. The video shows exactly that: approaching the bunny's spot, and pulling the special egg that sits behind the event boss. The catch the walkthrough highlights is the setup — you need a level six fire before the attempt makes sense, which is why most players who run straight to the bunny come back empty. This is a one-target video: no sweep, no list, just the bunny approach and the grab, so it stays short and dense.

## Key takeaways

- **Bring a level six fire.** The run treats level six fire as the entry condition — the grab behind the Evil Bunny punishes a low-level camp setup, and most failed attempts fail here, not at the bunny itself.
- **The egg sits behind the Evil Bunny, not on him.** Do not waste hits on the bunny expecting a drop. The special item is positioned behind the boss, and the walkthrough pauses the moment you need to turn around.
- **This is an event-only collectible.** Like the rest of the Easter eggs, the bunny spot exists only during the event window. The 2026 version of this trick will not work after the event rotates.
- **Do it on a night you already control.** Secret grabs go wrong when they collide with a Deer patrol or a cultist raid — pick a calm night after reading the [survival basics]({{< relref "/guide/how-to-play" >}}).
