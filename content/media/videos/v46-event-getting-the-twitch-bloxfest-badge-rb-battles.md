## What this video actually shows

Bloxfest is the Roblox-wide event tied to RB Battles, and 99 Nights in the Forest contributes its own badge toward the Glitch Helmet collection. This walkthrough covers the Twitch-side badge — the one that requires the cross-platform Bloxfest chain rather than pure in-game survival. The run shows what you actually have to do inside 99 Nights to unlock it, where the in-game trigger sits, and how the badge hands off to the next step on the road to the helmet. It is event-chain footage, not survival footage: expect short gameplay clips and a longer explanation of the event logic.

Bloxfest itself is Roblox's crossover event tied to RB Battles, so none of its badges make sense in isolation — each game contributes one puzzle piece, and players travel between games to collect them all. 99 Nights contributes two pieces total, and this video handles the Twitch-side half: the part of the chain that asks you to link your account or watch a stream rather than play more survival. The run also flags the trap that catches most people: finishing the game does not auto-unlock the badge. You have to claim it where the event UI asks.

## Key takeaways

- **This is the second Bloxfest badge from 99 Nights.** The event chains badges across multiple games — your job here is one specific badge, then you move on to the next game in the chain.
- **The Twitch side matters.** Part of this badge happens outside the game, on Twitch, so the most useful seconds of the walkthrough are where it shows the handoff between game and stream-side requirement.
- **Do not over-survive for it.** Bloxfest badges usually trigger on a specific in-game moment, not on reaching night 99. Watch for the exact trigger the run pauses on.
- **The Glitch Helmet is the real prize.** This is badge two of four — each badge you collect is one piece of the cross-event helmet, and this video tells you which piece 99 Nights provides.
