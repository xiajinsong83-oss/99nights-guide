## What the damage test shows

This is a numbers video: the author fights the same rifle against every major animal and tallies exactly how many shots each one eats. The lineup runs from the bunny up through the wolf, the alpha wolf, and finally the bear, so you get a damage ladder instead of a feeling. The useful part is watching the encounters themselves — a bear soaks up a small magazine before dropping, while a wolf goes down in barely any shots. That ratio decides fights. If you are mid-encounter and counting ammo in your head, knowing the bear-versus-wolf gap is the difference between reloading and running. The video does the counting for you, with footage of each kill so the shot tallies are visible rather than claimed.

## Takeaways you can use

- **The ammo math changes per target.** A wolf fight and a bear fight need completely different reserves. Watch the tallies before you wander into bear territory.
- **Bunny versus bear is the spread.** The low end of the ladder confirms the rifle is overkill on small game; the bear end tells you how much ammo you actually carry for the dangerous stuff.
- **Ammo is the resource that ends hunts.** The test implicitly shows that running dry mid-bear-fight is a real risk, not a hypothetical. Carry more than the tallies suggest.
- **The alpha wolf sits between wolf and bear.** If you have fought normal wolves, don't assume the alpha behaves by the same budget — the video shows the extra cost.
