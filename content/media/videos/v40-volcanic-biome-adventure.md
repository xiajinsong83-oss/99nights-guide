## What this video actually shows

A full tour of the volcanic biome update, narrated in Arabic, with survival tips layered over the gameplay. - **The tour is the content.** Entry routes, danger stretches, loot spots — the video shows the biome as geography rather than as a checklist.

- **Language is a smaller barrier than expected.** The gameplay footage and UI carry the tour; the Arabic commentary adds emphasis, not the instructions themselves.
