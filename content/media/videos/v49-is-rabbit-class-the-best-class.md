## What this video actually shows

Rabbit class is the meme pick that might actually be broken — it trades normal meat survival for an egg economy, and this video puts that claim under pressure. The run invests in Rabbit, grinds toward the 900-egg milestone, and shows what the carrot buffs actually look like: speed boosts, the buffed economy loop, and the catch the title hints at — a no-meat downside that changes how you feed yourself. It is a claim-testing video: "best class" is the hypothesis, and the run is the experiment.

The run also handles the part most meme-tier videos skip: what the egg economy actually costs in time. Nine hundred eggs sounds like a joke until you watch the grind segment, and the video's honest point is that the investment pays off only because the carrot buffs convert that eggs-per-hour rate into real survival power — speed, self-feeding, an economy that never touches the meat market. If you hate grinding, the conclusion is already clear; if you like speed builds, the footage makes the case.

## Key takeaways

- **The 900-egg milestone is the class's centerpiece.** Eggs are not flavor — they are the resource the whole Rabbit economy converts. The video spends real time showing the egg grind.
- **Carrot buffs translate to speed and economy perks.** Rabbit's upside is mobility and a self-sustaining food loop, shown in the run rather than listed on a card.
- **The no-meat downside is the real tradeoff.** No meat means no normal hunting income — watch how the run replaces it, because that replacement is the whole build.
- **"Best" means "for this playstyle."** The run's conclusion is honest: Rabbit is dominant at its own loop and awkward everywhere else.
