## What this video actually shows

The Taming Update added companions across the whole animal roster, and this video is the full reference: every animal that can be tamed, with timestamps for each recipe. The run works through the roster systematically — from the easy early animals up to the late-game ones — so you can build a companion collection instead of taming whatever you happen to meet. Like the egg-location videos, the format leans on timestamps: one long sweep, bookmarked by species, so you can jump to the animal you actually want.

Two things make the sweep more than a recipe reel. First, the flute tier required per animal is visible throughout — early animals unlock at a low flute, and the big names sit at the top of the ladder, so the video doubles as a progression guide for the taming system itself. Second, the approach to each animal is filmed the same way every time, which makes the differences comparable: who skittishes, who charges, who just needs snacks dropped nearby.

## Key takeaways

- **Every tame has its own recipe.** The sweep proves there is no universal snack — each animal wants specific food, and the timestamp list is your shopping sheet.
- **Timestamps make it a lookup, not a watch.** Open the description, find your animal, jump — do not sit through the whole roster on the first watch.
- **The Taming Update raised the flute's importance.** Companions exist on the whole roster now, so the Tamer's Flute upgrade ladder matters more than it used to.
- **Companion priority matters.** Not every tame is worth the recipe — the sweep lets you compare costs before you commit a stack of food.
