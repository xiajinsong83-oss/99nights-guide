## What this video actually shows

The Ice Bow in action: the newest weapon from the snow content, shot at range against the biome's threats. A showcase video, so the whole runtime is demonstration — draw, fire, watch the effect land. Its value is visual: you see the bow's projectile behavior, its range feel, and how the freeze-side effect plays against snow-zone enemies. - **The Ice Bow is a range option.** The showcase positions it as a weapon you keep at distance, not swap to in melee.
- **It ties to the snow content.** The bow comes out of the biome's reward loop — this is the payoff the [snow biome tour]({{< relref "/media/videos/v35-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-snow-biome" >}}) leads toward.

## Is it worth watching

Yes if you are deciding whether to grind the snow content for this bow — the showcase is the fastest way to see whether its playstyle fits before you invest. If you already own it, the clip adds little; the [weapons page]({{< relref "/advanced/weapons-tools" >}}) handles comparisons, and this video is the recruiting poster, not the manual. Watch it once at the decision moment, then move on.

The showcase also clarifies the bow's role against the rifle. Where the rifle is raw damage, the Ice Bow trades numbers for control — its value is slowing and freezing biome threats while you reposition, not deleting them outright. If your loadout already has a ranged damage answer, the bow fills the control slot. The demonstration makes that difference visible in a way a stats table cannot: you watch a target slow down instead of fall. For snow content specifically, freeze utility pairs with the Freeze debuff mechanics, which is why this bow and the biome released together.
