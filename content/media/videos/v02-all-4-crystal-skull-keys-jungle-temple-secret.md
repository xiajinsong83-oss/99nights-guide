## What this video shows

The Jungle Temple got a real glow-up in the Biome Refresher Part 2 update, and this run covers the whole key hunt: where each of the four Crystal Skull Keys spawns, how you cross the sinking stepping stones without a penalty, and what waits inside the temple once every key turns. That last part matters — a lot of guides stop at "find the keys," and the secret reward chamber is the reason the hunt exists. The video is filmed as one continuous trip through the jungle, so you see the terrain around each key spot instead of a map screenshot with a circle on it. For a quest this easy to half-finish, that continuity is what separates a useful walkthrough from a list of coordinates.

## Takeaways you can use

- **The four keys are spread across the jungle, not stacked.** The video visits each spawn in order, and you learn the landmarks around them. Rushing in blindly is how players miss the one tucked behind the ruins.
- **The stepping stones sink under you.** Hesitation costs here. The run crosses them in a direct line; pausing on a stone drops you into the hazard and burns the trip's momentum.
- **Keys are night-safe content only if you prep.** The hunt takes a full day cycle. Pack food, a spear, and repair wood before you leave camp, the way the day-gather / night-defend loop assumes.
- **The temple reward is the payoff.** This is content you can finish once and never need again, so take notes while watching. There is no reason to run the four-key route twice.
