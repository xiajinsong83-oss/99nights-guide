## What this video actually shows

A faster companion to the full sweep — this guide runs through quick taming recipes for every animal, one after another, without the long showcase footage of the Taming Update. The format is compact: animal, approach, food stack, tame moment, next animal. If the full sweep is the encyclopedia, this is the cheat sheet — the same roster, less scenery, less hunting footage, just the recipe per species and the moment the tame lands.

The tradeoff of the compact format is on display from the first segment: each animal gets roughly a minute, so the video assumes you already understand how taming works in general — the flute, the food stack, the patience. If this is your first encounter with the system, the first run will feel fast. The upside is that you end up with one screenshot per animal you can keep: approach, food, done, next.

## Key takeaways

- **Compact format means fast lookup.** Every animal gets its own short segment — navigate to your target species instead of watching the full roster.
- **Recipe per animal, shown on screen.** The food stacks are visible before each tame, which makes this better than a memory test when you are mid-hunt.
- **Approach differences are subtle.** Watch the short approach clips — animals do not all let you walk straight up, and the quick format still shows who gets skittish.
- **Pairs with the full sweep.** Use this video when you know what you want; use [the timestamped sweep]({{< relref "/media/videos/v52-taming-all-animals-how-to-tame-every-animal" >}}) when you want to browse the whole roster first.

## Is it worth watching

Yes as a reference video — it is the quick version of the taming roster, and shorter to navigate than the full sweep. The honest limit: compact segments mean less approach footage per animal, so for the hardest targets — the bear, especially — you still want the [dedicated bear guide]({{< relref "/media/videos/v51-how-to-tame-a-bear-level-3-flute-guide" >}}). Everything in the mid-tier, though, fits this cheat sheet perfectly, and that is most of the roster.
