## What this video actually shows

Bears are the toughest tame in the forest — they hit like a boss and they wander the dangerous biomes — and this guide breaks the taming into its two hard requirements: the Level 3 Tamer's Flute, and the exact food recipe. The run shows the flute upgrade first, then the tame attempt with the full recipe on screen: five salmon, five steak, five pumpkin. What the video adds over a written recipe is the approach itself — how to get close to a bear without becoming its dinner, and what the taming moment looks like when it actually works.

The inventory segment is worth pausing on: five salmon comes from fishing, five steak means a hunt, and five pumpkins means a farm plot — so the "simple recipe" quietly assumes a mid-game player who already owns all three food pipelines. The run also shows what happens if the bear aggros mid-attempt: break line of sight, drop the chase, come back when the animal calms. That pause saves you the corpse run most first attempts end in.

## Key takeaways

- **The Level 3 Tamer's Flute is the gate.** A lower-tier flute fails the attempt — this is the upgrade you work toward first, before hunting any bear.
- **The recipe is 5 salmon, 5 steak, 5 pumpkin.** Three food types, fixed counts. The video shows the inventory stack before the approach so you see exactly what to carry.
- **Approach risk is half the fight.** Bears do not let you walk up with snacks — watch how the run positions itself and why it waits.
- **A tamed bear is a late-game ally.** This is not an early-night project; the flute tier alone says you are well into the run.
