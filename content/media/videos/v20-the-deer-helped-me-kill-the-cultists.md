## What the wild night shows

The premise sounds impossible: the Deer usually commands cultists, so how is it helping you kill them? This video is the footage of that night. A cultist raid arrives, the Deer shows up, and instead of the two threats coordinating, they turn on each other. The player survives a chaotic night by doing almost nothing — watching the Deer tear through the raiders it would normally be leading. Beyond the novelty, the video is evidence for how the Deer's relationship with cultists actually works in practice. The usual model is "the Deer controls them." This night shows the leash can slip, and the footage explains why. It is not something to plan around, but it is a night to watch.

## Takeaways you can use

- **The Deer-cultist relationship is not always aligned.** Normally it commands raiders. This night shows the exception.
- **When they fight each other, stay out.** The player doesn't insert themselves into the crossfire. Let the chaos resolve and pick off survivors.
- **This is an anomaly, not a strategy.** Don't count on the Deer helping you on a raid night. It is a wild card, not a tactic.
- **Watch the night from cover.** The run survives by observing. Getting involved turns a one-sided fight into a three-way one.
