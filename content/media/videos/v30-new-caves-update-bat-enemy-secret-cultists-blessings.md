## What this video actually shows

A full underground run through the cave update. The creator enters the cave system, shows the new Bat enemies attacking in the dark, then something the surface players never expect: cultists hiding down there. The run ends at a blessing shrine where you pick one buff, which is the update's reward loop — risk the dark, fight the new threats, choose a permanent perk. The video's strength is that it plays the whole chain start to finish, so you see how entry, combat and blessing choice connect.

## Key takeaways

- **Bats are a new underground threat.** They are dark-zone enemies, so the usual "stay near the light" discipline matters down there more than on the surface.
- **Cultists went subterranean.** You already fight them on a raid timer above ground; finding them in the caves means the cultist content is no longer just your base's night problem.
- **Blessings are a choose-one reward.** The shrine hands you a buff, and the video shows the options rather than forcing one — pick around what your build already lacks.
- **The cave is a destination, not a basement.** It unlocks off the campfire progression, not by digging.

## Is it worth watching

Yes if the cave update is new to you — the blessing choice is the kind of moment where text can say "pick a buff" but the video shows the actual menu and the trade-off. Once you have claimed a blessing yourself, the video becomes optional; the [caves walkthrough]({{< relref "/media/videos/v32-how-to-get-through-the-caves" >}}) is the more reusable reference, and this one is the tour that sold you on going down in the first place.

A practical sequence: do the campfire level-3 unlock first, run the caves prep video, then play this one. That order keeps the shrine choice from feeling blind. Also note that the blessing you pick persists — this is not a per-trip buff you can swap freely, so the video's menu reading shapes your next stretch of progression. If your build is ranged, lean toward a buff that widens that gap rather than fixing a weakness you will not meet underground.
