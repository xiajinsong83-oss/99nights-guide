## What the Ram update coverage shows

This video is update coverage for the Ram — the goat-deer hybrid added to the lava biome. The Ram gets described as invincible for a reason: it does not behave like a normal wildlife fight. Instead of tanking or kiting it the way you would a bear, the video shows what actually happens when you engage, and what its charge does to structures. The key takeaway from the footage is that the Ram smashes walls — a direct lesson for any base built with open perimeter gaps. The run also connects the Ram to the Cultist King fight, since both threats share the late-stage lava biome content and the same prep standards. Watching it, you get a sense of what the update changed and what your camp needs to survive the new dynamic.

## Takeaways you can use

- **The Ram charges through walls.** This is the update's structural lesson. Any base with weak perimeter gaps gets punished by the Ram's charge.
- **"Invincible" means don't tank it.** The footage shows what happens when you try to trade blows. The answer is avoidance and pathing, not a damage race.
- **It lives in the lava biome.** You will meet the Ram alongside scorpions, lava hazards, and volcano content. Treat the biome trip as a combined challenge.
- **Ram and the King fight share prep.** The same gear, ammo, and food standards apply to both late threats. Don't prep for one in isolation.

For players who have unlocked the volcano biome and heard something new is in there. The video introduces the threat and shows its actual impact on walls, which is more useful than the patch notes. Skip it if you are nowhere near late-game content yet — the Ram is not something you meet on night 20. The wall-smashes footage is the practical lesson.
