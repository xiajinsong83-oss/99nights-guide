## What the boss guide walks through

The Cultist King is the boss at the end of the cultist raid line, and this video breaks the fight into the pieces that actually matter. Loadout first: a ranged weapon with a real ammo reserve, because the fight is not meant to happen at melee range. Position second: the doorway line-of-sight spot, where the King gets stuck firing through a choke while you shoot from cover. Movement third: kiting him around trees when the doorway logic breaks down — the boss pathing is slow enough that a tree between you and him is a real safety margin. The fight is shown start to finish, so you see each phase transition and what the player does differently at each stage. Reading about a line-of-sight spot is one thing; seeing the boss stuck at it is another.

## Takeaways you can use

- **Ranged gear or don't fight.** The guide assumes a ranged weapon and enough ammo to last the whole phase. Melee attempts are why most players lose this fight.
- **The doorway spot wins the fight.** Find the line-of-sight choke before you engage. The King losing sight of you for half the fight is half the battle.
- **Trees are your kiting route.** When you must move, circle obstacles deliberately. Standing in an open field against this boss is asking to get caught.
- **Raiders and the King overlap.** The fight happens during cultist activity — remember the raid schedule (every 3-4 days) when you plan the attempt.

## Who should watch

For players who have reached the Stronghold stage and keep losing to the King. The guide is specifically tuned to that fight, so you skip the generic survival advice. If cultists are not even a threat to you yet, come back once raids actually start landing. The doorway line-of-sight spot is the part that makes or breaks the attempt. Most players die because they try to fight in the open, not because they are under-geared. Watch the footage of the King stuck at the doorway, then copy that geometry before you bring ammo in. If the spot isn't working on your map, kite around trees — the video shows that fallback too.
