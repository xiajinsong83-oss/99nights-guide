## What the axe test covers

The Strong Axe is one of those upgrades everyone crafts and almost nobody tests properly. This video does the testing: the author swings it at rabbit, wolf, alpha wolf and polar bear, counting hits until each drops. The polar bear fight is the centerpiece — it is the toughest melee target on the list, and the video shows how many exchanges it actually takes. The payoff is knowing whether the axe is a real weapon or just a chopping tool you happened to bring along. Watching the counts roll in, the split becomes obvious: small game is trivial, wolves need a measured combo, and the polar bear is a commitment fight where every missed swing costs you.

## Takeaways you can use

- **Count the swings mid-fight.** The video makes the hit counts visible. If you know the polar bear's swing budget, you can decide to disengage before it runs out.
- **The axe is not free on wolves.** Early game, players sometimes assume an upgraded axe trivializes wildlife. The counts show otherwise for alpha wolves and up.
- **Melee versus ranged is a choice.** Compare these numbers with the [rifle shot counts]({{< relref "/media/videos/v10-how-many-rifle-shots-to-kill-bear-wolf-alpha" >}}) and the weapon class that suits your materials becomes obvious.
- **Polar bear territory needs prep.** A melee fight with a polar bear is a long one. The video implicitly tells you to bring food, not just a sharp tool.

## Who should watch

For players weighing whether to invest in the Strong Axe as their main weapon, or who keep losing melee fights they thought were easy. The hit tallies settle the question with footage, not claims. If you main a ranged build already, this is a nice-to-have reference rather than required viewing. What the footage makes obvious: the axe is a strong chopping tool first and a weapon second. Bring food before a bear fight, because the melee trade is what bleeds you.
