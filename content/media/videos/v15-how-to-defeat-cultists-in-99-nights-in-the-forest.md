## What the cultist breakdown shows

Cultists are the recurring night threat in this game, and this video is the behavioral study most players need before they start losing walls to them. It covers who they are — the worshippers who arrive on a raid cycle every 3-4 days — and how they behave once they show up: where they path, what they target first, and how groups split up around your camp. The footage runs through several raids so the pattern is visible instead of claimed. Knowing the 3-4 day cycle alone changes how you play: nights stop being random and become a schedule you can prep for. The video also shows how the Deer relates to cultists — it commands them, which is why the two threats often feel connected.

## Takeaways you can use

- **Raids arrive on a cycle.** Every 3-4 days, expect worshippers. Prep on the off days; don't rebuild walls under fire.
- **They target the campfire and the perimeter.** The pathing footage shows where they focus. Protecting the approach matters more than walling random sides.
- **Groups split around obstacles.** Walls and trees break their formation, which is why funneling works.
- **The Deer directs them.** When the Deer patrols at night with cultists, it is not a coincidence. The bigger threat controls the smaller ones.

For players who keep getting caught by surprise on raid nights. The behavioral breakdown turns raids from chaos into a pattern, and the cycle knowledge alone is worth the watch. Skip it if you already have raids figured out and just want the King fight.
