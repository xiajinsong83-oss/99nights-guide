## What the maze run demonstrates

Cultist raids are usually treated as a problem to survive. This video treats them as a resource — and makes the base so unraidable that 500-plus days of farming raids is the result. The build is a kill-funnel maze: walls and one-way channels that force raiders into a predictable path, where they die on schedule instead of reaching your campfire. The footage shows the maze from the builder's angle first (why each segment sits where it does), then from inside during an actual raid, so you see raiders marching into the funnel and getting picked off one by one. The "broken" label in the title is deserved: once the maze works, the raids stop being scary and start being the farm.

## Takeaways you can use

- **Force raiders into a line, don't scatter them.** The maze works because raiders become predictable. A wide open base turns every raid into a melee you have to personally manage.
- **One entrance, and only one.** Every extra gap in your perimeter is a place the maze logic breaks. The video plugs the camp against everything except the funnel.
- **Position your kill spot safely.** Raiders funneling in still shoot back; the player fires from a covered angle, not from the middle of the channel. Copy that geometry, not just the walls.
- **This is a late-game build.** The maze costs a lot of wall segments and patience. Don't try it on night 10 — it is an upgrade for a base that already survives normal raids.

## Who should watch

For mid-to-late players sick of repairing walls after every raid night. The payoff — raids become free loot instead of damage events — is worth the build time. Skip it if you are still learning the basic loop; the maze presumes a base that already works and materials to burn. The 500-plus day streak in the title is not a flex, it's evidence the build is reproducible. The one thing the video can't give you is patience: a maze base takes time to lay out, and players who rush the construction end up with a half-finished funnel that doesn't work. Build it on a quiet day.
