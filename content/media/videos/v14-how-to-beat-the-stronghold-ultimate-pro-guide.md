## What the pro guide covers

The Stronghold is the late-stage challenge this game builds toward, and this guide works backward from it. It starts with the best early weapons — what to build in your first nights that still matters 60 nights later. Then the prep list for the Stronghold itself: gear, food, ammo, and camp state the night before. Then the boss fight step-by-step, with the player narrating each move as it happens. The final stretch ties it to the broader goal of surviving all 99 nights, so the guide reads like a campaign plan rather than a single fight. That is its strength. Most Stronghold videos assume you arrived ready; this one explains how you got ready.

## Takeaways you can use

- **Early weapon choices have late payoff.** The guide names what to craft first because those same choices carry you to the Stronghold. Don't skip the opener.
- **Prep the night before, not at the door.** Food, ammo, and a repaired base the night before the attempt are the difference between a clean fight and a retreat.
- **The boss fight is staged.** Watching the step-by-step, you see what changes between phases and what the player does to match it. Memorize the phase tells.
- **The goal is the 99-night run, not just the boss.** This guide treats the Stronghold as one milestone in a full survival push. Plan your resources over the whole arc.
