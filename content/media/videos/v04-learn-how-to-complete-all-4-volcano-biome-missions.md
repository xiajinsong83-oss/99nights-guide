## What the run covers

The volcanic biome is one of the content zones players reach late, and its mission board hides a lot behind the first trip. This video runs all four volcano missions in one session: the lava sacrifice step, the 100-scorpion hunt, the ammo tower objective, and the fourth mission alongside the hidden secrets most players miss. Footage matters here because the volcano is punishing — stepping wrong on the lava hazards, getting swarmed by scorpion poison, or running out of ammo mid-tower are all things you see the player recover from, not just narrate over. By the end you have a full picture of the lava biome loop: what it asks of you, what it gives back (the inferno gear like the lava sword and lava potion), and where the optional stuff sits.

## Takeaways you can use

- **Scorpion poison is the mission's real enemy.** The 100-scorpion hunt sounds like a grind number, but the poison ticks are what end runs. Bring the right food and don't tank swarms.
- **The ammo tower mission assumes you stocked up.** Showing up short on ammo turns a defense mission into a retreat. Load before you enter the biome, not after.
- **Lava sacrifices cost materials on purpose.** You are giving up resources to progress. Don't dump your repair wood or wall stock into sacrifices you planned to use tonight.
- **Keep an eye out between missions.** The hidden secrets sit off the main path; the video pauses at each one, and those stops are worth watching even if you do the missions another day.

## Who should watch

For players who have unlocked the volcano but keep coming home empty-handed. The video turns four vague mission names into a day-planned checklist. Skip it if you already cleared the biome — the secrets are one-time content, and re-watching won't reveal new drops. If you are sitting on the edge of unlocking it, hold off: the volcano is content for a run that already has steady ammo, food, and repair wood. Walking in under-geared is the reason most first attempts fail, and this video won't fix that for you.
