## What the walkthrough covers

The missing-kid quest is the long thread running through your first ten nights, and a lot of players ignore it because the red arrow feels random. This video fixes that. It starts at the bulletin board and walks one full rescue cycle: reading the board, chasing the arrow clue by clue, handling the guardian that shows up when you reach the kid, and then carrying the kid back to camp while your movement speed drops. The value here is visual. A text guide can tell you to follow the arrow, but only footage shows where the board actually sits relative to camp, how the arrow refreshes after each clue, and which routes the guardians watch on the way back. If you have ever stood in a forest clearing wondering which way the arrow actually wants you to go, this is the video that shows the loop working start to finish.

## Takeaways you can use

- **Start at the bulletin board, not the wilderness.** The board posts the current clue, and the red arrow tracks only that clue. Chase the arrow to the next clue, then the next, until the kid appears. Wandering off on your own finds nothing.
- **The carry is the dangerous part.** Moving a kid slows you down, and guardians spawn along the return path. The player here circles around them instead of fighting head-on, and that pacing is the whole lesson.
- **Rescues pay off in skipped nights.** Finishing all four rescues, together with expanding your campfire, is what lets you jump multiple nights in one cycle. It is the quest side of the [Skip 8 Days per Night]({{< relref "/media/videos/v03-how-to-skip-8-days-per-night-progress-guide" >}}) video.
- **Do it by daylight.** The run assumes no wandering at night: spear in hand, hunger bar full, back before dark. Rescue after sundown and the Deer and the guardians compound into one bad night.
