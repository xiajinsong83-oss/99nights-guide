## What this video actually shows

This is the workhorse Bloxfest video: the second badge in the Glitch Helmet chain, and the one that costs real in-game effort. The walkthrough covers radar crafting, the 101-nights requirement, and the "scan everything" badge path — meaning you are not clicking a single trigger, you are building toward a badge over a whole survival run. The run shows the radar craft, what you scan with it, and where the 101-night milestone sits in the chain. Of the two Bloxfest videos here, this is the one that overlaps with your normal survival planning.

The radar is the tool that turns a vague "scan everything" requirement into a checklist — the video shows the craft bench, the ingredients stacked, and then the scanning runs that actually count. A subtle point the run makes: not every scan spot registers on the first pass. The scanning requirement is finicky enough that the video flags which biomes to sweep twice, which is the detail most players miss when they improvise their own route.

## Key takeaways

- **The badge needs a crafted Radar.** Crafting comes first — the "scan everything" part is useless until you have the radar built, and the video walks the craft before the scanning.
- **Finishing 101 nights is the gate.** Past the 99-night survival run, the badge asks for one more night cycle. Plan for a stable long-term base before you aim at this.
- **"Scan everything" means everywhere.** The scanning requirement spans biomes — the video's list of scan spots is the part to screenshot, because re-watching to find them is annoying.
- **This is badge 2 of 4.** One more badge down, two to go across other games; keep the event chain's other requirements in mind before you invest the 101 nights.

Yes if the Glitch Helmet is on your list — this badge is the grindy one, and the radar-craft plus scan-spots list saves you from guessing where to point the device.
