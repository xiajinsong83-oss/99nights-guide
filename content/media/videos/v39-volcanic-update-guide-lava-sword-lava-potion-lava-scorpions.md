## Key takeaways

- **Lava Sword is the melee payoff.** The video treats it as the craft that makes the biome's close fights survivable, not a trophy.
- **Lava Potion is preparedness.** Brewing it before the trip, not mid-combat — the video shows the prep order, which is the tactical part.
- **Scorpions inflict poison.** The tactics section is about handling the poison stack, not just the damage — pace the fights so the status does not snowball.
- **Lava itself is a hazard.** The biome's ground rules punish bad routing as much as its mobs do.

Yes — this is the combat-focused volcano reference, and the scorpion poison tactics are the kind of detail text summarizes poorly. If your volcano plan is the mission chain, pair it with [v04]({{< relref "/media/videos/v04-learn-how-to-complete-all-4-volcano-biome-missions" >}}); if it is just gearing up, this video alone covers the essential craft-and-fight loop. The scorpion poison section is the part to study most closely. Poison stacks punish extended fights, so the tactics the video demonstrates are about pacing — engage, disengage, let the status settle, rather than trading hits until one side falls. The Lava Sword and Lava Potion exist to make that pacing affordable: the sword shortens fights, the potion absorbs mistakes. One read the video supports: do not craft the gear after you arrive at the biome. Brew and forge before the trip, because inside the lava zone is the worst place to realize you came unprepared.
