## What this video actually shows

The Easter event in 99 Nights in the Forest hides eggs all over the map, and this walkthrough is the sweep video — it starts by claiming the free Deer Egg, then walks from the home camp out through every hidden egg spawn the event added. The list on screen covers the basketball egg, the volcano egg, the virus egg and the alien egg, plus the smaller ones that blend into the scenery. What a text guide cannot give you is the exact look of each spot: the basketball hoop on a specific wall, the egg tucked against the volcano rocks, the alien-themed corner that only exists during the event window. If you have been running around the forest clicking everything and still missing two eggs, this is the video that closes the gap.

## Key takeaways

- **Claim the Deer Egg first.** It is the event's headline free reward and it sits in a fixed, easy-to-find spot — grab it before you start the long sweep, so you do not re-walk the same paths twice.
- **The eggs span multiple biomes.** This is not a camp-area hunt. The basketball, volcano, virus and alien eggs are spread out across the map, so plan the route by biome rather than by how many eggs you have left.
- **Hidden eggs are event-timed.** Spawns like the alien corner only exist while the Easter event is live. The walkthrough is only useful inside that window — after the event rotates out, the spots go cold.
- **Watch with the sound on.** Half of these spots are unmarked; the commentary points out when to peel off the main path, which is exactly the part people miss on their own.
