## What this video actually shows

Hard mode in 99 Nights is the survival run with the rifts active — corrupted trees, infected animals, a forest that fights back harder — and this video compiles ten pro tips for beating it. The run shows the hard-mode reality: the rifts that must be deactivated, the day loop under corruption pressure, and the specific discipline changes a normal run does not need. The ten tips cover the prep before activation and the mid-run decisions during a hard-mode night.

The tips themselves split cleanly: prep advice for before you flip the hardmode lever, and discipline advice for after. The prep list is the one to screenshot — what level your campfire needs to be, what food stockpile you want, what base integrity should look like before the corruption starts. The discipline tips cover the mid-run decisions normal play never tests: when to abandon a corrupted resource node instead of grinding it, and how to reroute your night defense around an active rift.

- **Deactivate the rifts.** The headline task: hard mode's corruption is channeled through rifts, and shutting them down is the lever the tips keep returning to.
