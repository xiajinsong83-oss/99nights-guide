## What this video actually shows

If the sweep video is the route, this one is the lookup table: a complete egg-location guide built around timestamps. The creator walks every egg added by the Easter update — basketball, lightning, boiled, glowing, volcano and the rest — and marks a timestamp for each one in the description, so you can jump straight to whichever egg you have not found yet. The strength of this format is also its limit: it assumes you know the map well enough to recognize a spot from ten seconds of footage, and it does not slow down to explain what each biome entrance looks like or how to reach it safely.

## Key takeaways

- **Use the timestamp list, not the full watch.** With a dozen eggs, the smart way to watch this is scanning the description list, identifying your missing egg names, and jumping to those two timestamps instead of sitting through the whole roster.
- **The egg names are visual puns.** "Boiled", "glowing", "lightning" — each egg looks like its name. When you are hunting on your own, scan for the visual cue first instead of checking every corner.
- **The volcano egg demands prep.** Reaching the volcano area means crossing lava hazards, so watch that segment with an eye on what gear you need — this video assumes you already own the route.
- **One watch-through is still worth it before the event ends.** Even if you have all the eggs, the full sweep shows the spawn logic: where event designers tuck the things that are easiest to miss.
