Most codes in 99 Nights in the Forest are redeemed in the **Lobby** with just four taps. One special code — **yay fishing** — works differently, and it is explained at the bottom of this page.

## Redeeming lobby codes

1. **Launch the game** and stay in the Lobby. Do not join a server yet.
2. **Open the shop / gem menu** — click the **diamond icon** in the **bottom-left corner** of the screen.
3. **Open the codes window** — press the blue **Codes** button in the **bottom-right** of that menu.
4. **Type the code** exactly as listed and hit **Submit**.
5. Rewards (Gems, Flames, etc.) are granted instantly — check your balance to confirm.

{{< notice >}}Codes are **not case-sensitive**, but the spelling must be exact — including the space in **yay fishing**. If a code is expired, the game simply says it's invalid or already used. That is how you know it's dead, not that you typed it wrong.{{< /notice >}}

## The special chat code: yay fishing

**yay fishing** is NOT redeemed in the lobby. To get its 2 Gems:

1. Upgrade your **Campfire to level 2**.
2. Craft/obtain the **fishing rod**.
3. Go to a fishing spot and cast the rod so it sits **in the water**.
4. Press **/** to open chat and type:

   `yay fishing`

5. Press enter — the 2 Gems spawn instantly.

The space between "yay" and "fishing" is required. Try it at any fishing spot with the rod in the water.

## Why codes expire

Developers retire codes with game updates to keep the reward economy balanced. When a code dies, we move it to the **Expired Codes** archive on the [Codes page](/codes/) so players don't waste time on outdated lists from other sites.
