## Why this list is shorter than the ones on YouTube

Roblox code farming channels make their money from long lists, so they pad with codes that expired months ago, invent "secret" codes, or reupload the same three strings under new thumbnails. A dead code in 99 Nights does nothing visible: the window just says invalid or already used, and you assume you misspelled it. We would rather show four live codes than forty fake ones.

Two things make this list behave differently from most games:

- **Codes arrive with updates.** `awardwinner_yay` showed up inside the September 19 Class Shop Revamp patch notes, and `forestwakesup26` launched with the June 13 Forest Wakes Up event. There is no monthly code schedule; codes ride on content patches.
- **One code is not a lobby code.** `yay fishing` does not work in the lobby at all. It is typed into the in-game chat box while your fishing rod sits in the water, and it quietly gives 2 Diamonds as long as your campfire is at least level 2. People type it in the lobby chat, get nothing, and call us liars. We are not lying; your fire is level 1.

## How redemption actually works

For every lobby code: launch the game and stay in the **lobby** (do not jump into a server), click the **diamond icon** in the bottom-left corner, press the blue **Codes** button, type the code, and hit Submit. Diamonds and Flames land instantly. The full click-by-click walkthrough, including screenshots of the menu order, lives on the [How to Redeem Codes]({{< relref "/codes/redeem" >}}) page.

A few habits that save time:

- **Case does not matter.** Type `AfterParty`, `afterparty`, `AFTERPARTY` — all fine. The spelling has to be exact, but capitalization does not.
- **The space in `yay fishing` is real.** The code contains a literal space between the two words. Remove it and nothing happens. This is the single most failed redemption in the game.
- **Expired codes tell the truth.** If the window says invalid or already used after a correct spelling, the code is dead. Do not retype it ten times. We move confirmed-dead codes to the expired table below.

## When codes die

Developers retire codes to keep the Diamond economy balanced, especially around events. `happyhalloween` worked during the 2025 Halloween event for 10 Candy and died when the event ended — that is the normal lifecycle, and it tells you the event codes in this game are seasonal. Expect a fresh batch around Halloween 2026, since the devs have already teased Halloween plans in the lobby, plus whatever rides the upcoming October 4 Helping Happy quest.

## What the Flames in these rewards mean

Three of the four current codes pay out Diamonds **plus Flames** — Frog, Thanksgiving, Fairy and Pet variants in rotation. Flames attach to your gear and reshape how it behaves, which is why collecting them matters more as the run goes on. The full breakdown sits on the [Modifier Flames & Fire Offerings]({{< relref "/advanced/modifier-flames" >}}) page; the short version is that a mid-tier weapon with the right Flame attached beats a plain top-tier weapon without one.
