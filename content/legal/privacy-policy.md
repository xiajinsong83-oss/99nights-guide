**99 Nights Guide Hub** ("we", "us") operates the website at `https://portalaser.com`. This page explains what information is collected when you visit the site and how it is used.

## Information we collect

- **No account data.** The site does not require registration, and we do not collect names, email addresses or payment information from visitors.
- **Feedback you send.** If you send feedback through the contact or feedback form, the message (including any name you include) is sent to us by email and used only to respond and improve the site. It is never sold or shared.
- **Server and analytics logs.** Cloudflare, our hosting provider, and Cloudflare Web Analytics collect standard technical data (IP address, browser type, approximate location, pages visited) for security, performance and audience measurement. This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.

## Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies:

- **Cloudflare** may set strictly-necessary cookies to keep the site working and secure.
- **Embedded YouTube videos** may set cookies in line with Google's privacy practices when you watch them.
- **Google AdSense** (if ads are enabled on this site) may use cookies to serve ads, including personalised ads based on your browsing. You can opt out of personalised advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), or visit [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info) for more options.

## Children's privacy

99 Nights in the Forest is a Roblox game, and some of our visitors are minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where personalised advertising is not appropriate, ads shown on this site may be non-personalised in accordance with Google's policies for content directed at children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Third-party services

- **Cloudflare** — hosting, CDN and analytics.
- **YouTube (Google)** — embedded guide videos.
- **Google AdSense** — advertising, when enabled.

Each service operates under its own privacy policy.

## Your choices

- Block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
- Opt out of personalised ads via Google Ads Settings.
- Contact us at any time with questions about your data.
