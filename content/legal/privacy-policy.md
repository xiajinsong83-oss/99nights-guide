**99 Nights Guide Hub** ("we", "us") runs the site at `https://www.portalaser.com`. This page explains what information is collected when you visit, how it is used, and what control you have over it.

## Information we collect

- **No account data.** The site needs no registration, and we do not collect names, email addresses or payment details from visitors.
- **Feedback you send.** If you use the contact or feedback form, your message reaches us by email and is used only to respond and improve the site. It is never sold or shared.
- **Server and analytics logs.** Our hosting and analytics provider collects standard technical data — IP address, browser type, approximate location, pages visited — for security, performance and rough audience measurement. This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.

## Cookies

The site uses minimal cookies:

- Strictly necessary cookies from our hosting provider keep the site secure and working.
- Embedded YouTube videos may set cookies in line with Google's privacy practices when you watch them.
- Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads, including personalised ads based on your browsing. You can opt out at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or learn more at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

## Children's privacy

Some visitors are minors, since the game we cover is played by younger Roblox players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and where personalised advertising is not appropriate, non-personalised ads may be shown under Google's policies. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Third-party services

- **Cloudflare** — hosting, CDN and analytics.
- **YouTube (Google)** — embedded guide videos.
- **Google AdSense** — advertising, when enabled.

Each provider operates under its own privacy policy.

## Your choices

Block or delete cookies through your browser settings, opt out of personalised ads at Google Ads Settings, or write to us any time with questions about your data. Feedback you send reaches us by email and is used only to respond and improve the site — it is never sold, shared or turned into an account.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The current version always lives on this page. We do not sell visitor data, and feedback messages are used only to respond and improve the guides.

## Data we do not hold

We do not store account logins, passwords or payment details. Embedded YouTube videos and ad cookies run under Google's own policies, and you can opt out of personalised ads at any time.

Third-party cookies from ad networks and embedded video players may still be set in your browser. You can clear or block them through your browser settings, and you can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. We do not control those cookies and cannot turn them off for you.
