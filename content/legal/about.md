**99 Nights Guide Hub** is a fan-made guide website for **99 Nights in the Forest**, the co-op survival horror game on Roblox.

## What we do

- **Working codes** — updated monthly and re-verified whenever the game updates.
- **Survival guides** — day-by-day routes for your first 10 nights, campfire and base upgrades.
- **Classes, monsters and advanced systems** — tier lists, counter strategies, crafting, fishing, taming and hard mode.
- **Video archive** — a growing library of quest walkthroughs, boss guides and update coverage.

## What we believe

- Guides should be **tested and honest** — if a number is not yet verified in-game, we say so.
- Updates should be **regular** — a guide site that goes stale is not useful.
- The community's voice matters — see our [Player Feedback]({{< relref "/feedback" >}}) page.
