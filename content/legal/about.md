**99 Nights Guide Hub** is a fan-made guide site for **99 Nights in the Forest**, the co-op survival game on Roblox made by Grandma's Favourite Games. The game's recent movie tie-in brought a flood of new players into servers that had been running on word-of-mouth for months, and there was no single place that had the codes, the routes and the boss counters in one honest place. We built this site to be that place.

## What the site covers

- **Working codes** — re-verified on every game update, with expired codes kept on display so you can tell a live list from a padded one.
- **Survival guides** — the day-by-day route for your first 10 nights, campfire upgrade order, and base layout.
- **Classes, monsters and advanced systems** — tier list, the Deer counter, weapons and tools, Flames and Fire Offerings, fishing and taming, and the Forest Rage hard-mode checklist.
- **Video archive** — a curated library of quest walkthroughs, boss guides and biome deep dives.

## Who writes it

The site is written and maintained by the **PortalAser Team**, a small group of players who got tired of wiping the same runs and decided to write down what worked. We are not the developers. We test what we can in live runs, and when a number has not been verified on the current patch, the page says so instead of inventing a table.

## How often we update

Codes get re-checked against every patch, and the game update log refreshes automatically every 12 hours from the official Roblox game page. Prices and upgrade costs move with balance patches — the September 2026 Class Shop Revamp touched a lot of economy numbers — so our pages treat strategy as judgment and cost lists as something to confirm in-game.

## What we believe

A guide site that goes stale is worse than no guide at all. Guides should be tested and honest, the community's corrections should land on the site fast, and expired codes should never sit on a live list pretending to work.
